Privacy Policy for WorkoutTracker

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

1. Data Collection

When you log in with Facebook, we only collect your public profile information (Name) and Email address. We do not post anything to your Facebook wall or access your friends list.

2. Data Usage

Your data is strictly used to create an account in WorkoutTracker so you can securely save and view your personal workout history.

3. Data Deletion Instructions

According to Facebook platform rules, we provide a Data Deletion process. If you want to delete your activity and data from WorkoutTracker, you can:

  1. Go to your Facebook profile settings.
  2. Navigate to "Settings & Privacy" > "Settings" > "Apps and Websites".
  3. Find "WorkoutTracker" and click "Remove".
  4. To delete your workout data completely from our database, please contact the developer or use the "Delete Account" option inside the app (if available).
